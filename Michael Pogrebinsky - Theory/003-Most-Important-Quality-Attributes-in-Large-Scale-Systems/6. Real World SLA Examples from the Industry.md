### What to Look For When Reading an SLA

1. **The SLOs:** for example, the promised availability (how many nines) for each service
2. **How it is measured (the SLI):** for example, the percentage of successful requests in a month
3. **The penalties:** usually service credits, a percentage of the monthly bill given back when the promise is missed
4. **Exclusions:** situations the promise does not cover, such as planned maintenance

## Short Note

- Real SLAs to read: **AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, GitHub Enterprise and Atlassian**.
- In each one, look for the **SLOs** (the promised nines), the **SLI** (how it is measured), the **penalties** (usually service credits) and the **exclusions**.
